# The scene: sources and references

What this system was built from, for reference imagery and fact-checking.

## Origin and meaning

- The term joins the Frutiger typeface with Windows Aero. It was coined by Sofi Xian (also credited as Sofi Lee) and named with Froyo Tam of the Consumer Aesthetics Research Institute (CARI), and launched publicly in a Facebook group on Jan 18, 2018. CARI dates the style to about 2004 to 2013 and lists skeuomorphic UI, glossy and transparent materials, humanist sans-serifs, and aurora, bokeh and macro-grass photography. [CARI](https://cari.institute/aesthetics/frutiger-aero), [CARI history](https://cari.institute/history), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frutiger_Aero)
- Wikipedia's summary: "a utopia where efficiency and the environment coexist."
- "There's a lot of hopefulness in this aesthetic that Y2K doesn't have" (Amanda Brennan). Evan Collins of CARI links it to tech becoming approachable. [Dazed, Feb 2023](https://www.dazeddigital.com/life-culture/article/58103/1/what-is-frutiger-aero-aesthetic-tiktok-msn-messenger-windows-vista-noughties)
- The Guardian (Dec 2023) reads the revival as a reaction against the AI boom, and reports the TikTok hashtag at about 270 million views. [via inkl](https://www.inkl.com/news/frutiger-aero-the-windows-screen-saver-design-trend-taking-tiktok-by-storm)
- Creative Bloq calls it a reaction to "the coldness of modern design." [Creative Bloq, Jan 2024](https://www.creativebloq.com/news/frutiger-aero-aesthetic-resurgance)
- The Frutiger Aero Archive points to the Korean stock agency Asadal as the source of much of the utopian imagery, and flags "green grassy plains with tall buildings" as greenwashing. [Archive history](https://frutigeraeroarchive.org/history)

## Products the vocabulary is drawn from

- Wii and DSi: the TV-channel metaphor, rounded tiles and a big clock; system font Rodin NTLG. [30pin](https://www.30pin.com/features/wii-tv/), [Iwata Asks](https://iwataasks.nintendo.com/interviews/wii/wii_channels/0/0/), [NIWA fonts](https://niwanetwork.org/wiki/List_of_Nintendo_system_fonts)
- PS3 XMB: a translucent wave whose color follows the month and brightness follows the time of day. [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XrossMediaBar)
- Vista wallpapers by Hamad Darwish, shot on the Oregon coast and in the Rogue Valley. [Interview](https://istartedsomething.com/20070203/interview-hamad-darwish/)
- Windows Live Messenger 2009 "scenes" (header art like Daisy Hill and Cherry Blossoms). [Wallpaper wiki](https://windowswallpaper.miraheze.org/wiki/Windows_Live_Messenger)
- iPhone OS icon gloss, which the system added automatically unless an app opted out. [Apple QA1686](https://developer.apple.com/library/archive/qa/qa1686/_index.html)
- Aqua: "when you saw it you wanted to lick it" (Steve Jobs). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aqua_(user_interface))
- Zune's cropped oversized Segoe marks the move toward Frutiger Metro. [istartedsomething](https://istartedsomething.com/20071114/zegoe-zune-font/)

## Sub-styles

Frutiger Eco (renewables, green Earth), Dark Aero (black, indigo and cyan glows), Technozen ("cold, sterile… yet cozy and cute": Wii, DS), Frutiger Aurora, Vectorgarden (vector swirls, flowers, butterflies), Four Colors (iPod silhouette ads), Helvetica Aqua Aero (sea, bubbles, dolphins), and Frutiger Metro. [Aesthetics Wiki](https://aesthetics.fandom.com/wiki/Frutiger_Aero)
