Tileable backgrounds for `background-repeat`.

- pinstripe-light: 8px tile. Technozen and Aqua panels in Light and Technozen.
- pinstripe-dark: the same tile for Dark.
- caustics: 256px water-light tile for water surfaces and headers.
- chrome-stripes: 64px Y2K brushed-chrome band. Accents only.
- dot-grid: 16px faint grid for canvases.
- honeycomb-glass: white hex lines to layer over glass at low opacity.
